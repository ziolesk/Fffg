Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026 · Bei Poa Campus Marketplace

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information Bei Poa collects, how we use it, and what choices you have. We aim to be clear and honest so you know where you stand.

1. Who we are

Bei Poa is a campus marketplace platform. For data protection purposes, we are the organisation responsible for deciding how your personal data is used on this service. Technical hosting and database services are provided with the help of third parties such as Supabase and our website host (for example Render).

2. Information we collect

We may collect:

  • Account information – name, email address, password (stored securely), university, phone/WhatsApp number, and optional bio.
  • Listing information – titles, descriptions, prices, photos (via URLs you provide), condition, category, and contact details you choose to show.
  • Usage information – actions such as posting, reporting, logging in, and basic technical data (for example browser type) needed to run and secure the service.
  • Reports – when you or someone else submits a report about a listing or user, we store the reason and details you provide.
  • Login providers – if you sign in with Google, we receive basic profile information (such as name and email) from Google according to your Google settings.

We do not intentionally collect payment card numbers. Payments happen directly between users outside Bei Poa.

3. How we use your information
  • To create and manage your account
  • To show your listings to other users and allow contact for buying/selling
  • To moderate content, review reports, and keep the community safer
  • To improve the platform and fix problems
  • To communicate important service messages (for example security or policy updates)
  • To comply with the law when we are required to

We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

4. Legal bases (where applicable)

We process your data because:

  • You asked us to provide the service (contract / terms you accept)
  • We have a legitimate interest in running a safe marketplace and preventing fraud
  • We must meet legal obligations when they apply
  • You gave consent, where consent is needed (for example optional marketing, if we ever add it)
5. Who we share data with
  • Other users – when you list an item, other logged-in users can see the listing details and the contact information you provide for that listing.
  • Service providers – such as Supabase (database and authentication) and our website host. They process data only to provide their services to us.
  • Authorities – if the law requires us to disclose information, or if we need to protect users from serious harm or crime.

Moderators and admins on Bei Poa can access user and listing data needed to do their job (for example handling reports and bans).

6. How long we keep data

We keep your account and listing data while your account is active and as long as needed to operate the service. If your account is banned or deleted, we may keep limited records (for example to prevent repeat abuse or to meet legal requirements) for a reasonable period.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures such as encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure authentication. No system is 100% safe. Please use a strong password and do not share your login details. Meet buyers/sellers carefully and never send money to strangers without proper checks.

8. Your rights and choices

Depending on applicable law, you may be able to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information (you can edit much of it in your Profile)
  • Request deletion of your account
  • Object to or restrict certain processing

To exercise these rights, contact us through the platform support channels. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Bei Poa is aimed at university students and adults. It is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

10. International processing

Our service providers may process data in data centres in different countries. Where data leaves your country, we rely on appropriate safeguards offered by those providers and applicable law.
